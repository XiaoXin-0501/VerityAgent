# 册-04 · 心血管与血液疾病路径（犬猫）

## 4.1 充血性心力衰竭（左心衰竭）

**常见症状**
- 呼吸急促、呼吸困难、肺水肿；可有咳嗽、运动不耐受；猫常见胸腔积液、食欲下降、四肢末端发凉与低体温；少数可晕厥。
- 睡眠呼吸率（SRR）可作为居家监测指标；静息或睡眠状态下呼吸频率持续升高提示心源性呼吸受累，须就医评估。

**常见病因**
- 瓣膜返流（如二尖瓣返流）、扩张型心肌病、二尖瓣腱索断裂；猫以肥厚型心肌病等舒张功能障碍为主。

**处理原则**
- 控制水肿与积液：利尿减轻前负荷；扩血管降低后负荷；正性肌力 / 血管舒张药改善心功能；神经内分泌调节（ACE 抑制剂、醛固酮拮抗剂、β 阻断剂等按病情选用）。
- 缺氧时给氧；大量胸腔积液或腹水影响呼吸时穿刺排液；重症、心源性休克在监护下静脉用药。
- 监测肾功能、电解质与血压；长期用药者定期复查；严重心衰适度限钠，无症状或轻中度心衰限钠需谨慎。

**涉及药物**
- 利尿：呋塞米、托拉塞米、噻嗪类、螺内酯 / 依普利酮。
- 正性肌力 / 扩血管：匹莫苯丹、多巴酚丁胺、多巴胺、米力农、地高辛。
- 神经内分泌调节与扩血管：ACE 抑制剂（依那普利、贝那普利等）、肼屈嗪、氨氯地平、西地那非、阿替洛尔等。
- 营养补充：牛磺酸、左旋肉碱、Omega-3 脂肪酸。

**相关药物**：〔药-06·匹莫苯丹 `pimobendan`〕、〔药-06·呋塞米 `furosemide`〕、〔药-06·螺内酯+贝那普利 `spironolactone-benazepril`〕、〔药-06·依那普利 `enalapril`〕
## 4.2 右心衰竭

**常见症状**
- 颈静脉怒张、肝肿大、胸腔积液、心包积液、腹水、外周水肿；犬较易出现腹水，猫右心衰相对少见。

**处理原则**
- 治疗原发病（三尖瓣返流、肺动脉高压、心包填塞等）；利尿与必要时穿刺排液；肺动脉高压按评估使用血管扩张剂。

**涉及药物**
- 利尿：呋塞米、螺内酯；扩血管与肺动脉高压用药：氨氯地平、西地那非。

**相关药物**：〔药-06·匹莫苯丹 `pimobendan`〕
## 4.3 心律失常与心源性晕厥

**常见症状**
- 晕厥、乏力、心率过快或过慢；可与瓣膜病、心肌病或心肌损伤并存。

**处理原则**
- 依心电图与病因评估；急性血流动力学不稳定时监护下抢救；抗心律失常方案须专科制定。

**涉及药物**
- 抗心律失常与强心苷：利多卡因、阿替洛尔、索他洛尔、地尔硫䓬、地高辛。

## 4.4 贫血

**常见症状**
- 黏膜苍白、乏力、运动不耐受、心率呼吸加快；急性失血可见休克；慢性贫血症状相对缓和。

**分类与处理方向**
- 按网织红细胞分为再生性（失血、溶血）与非再生性（肾性、骨髓抑制、慢性疾病）。
- 先找并去除病因：外伤 / 寄生虫 / 胃肠出血导致的失血；免疫介导性溶血；肾性贫血；骨髓或营养因素（铁、维生素 B12、叶酸缺乏）。
- 重度贫血或休克需输血 / 血浆制品支持；肾性贫血用促造血药物；营养缺乏相应补充。

**涉及药物**
- 促造血：依泊汀 α、达贝泊汀 α、Molidustat（猫肾性贫血）、钴胺素、叶酸、铁剂。

## 4.5 出血与凝血障碍

**常见症状**
- 皮肤瘀斑、血肿、鼻出血、牙龈出血、尿血、便血；关节 / 肌肉内出血；术后渗血不止。

**常见病因**
- 血小板减少或功能障碍、凝血因子缺乏（血管性血友病、血友病、肝衰竭）、抗凝血灭鼠药中毒、弥散性血管内凝血（DIC）。

**处理原则**
- 先做凝血与血小板检查明确环节；止血同时治疗原发病；必要时输血 / 血浆 / 冷沉淀补充凝血因子或血小板。
- 抗凝血灭鼠药中毒以维生素 K1 为基础，重症配合血浆；纤溶亢进时用抗纤溶药。

**涉及药物**
- 止血：去氨加压素、维生素 K1、血浆 / 冷沉淀 / 血小板制品、氨基己酸、氨甲环酸。

## 4.6 血栓性疾病（抗血栓）

**常见方向**
- 动脉血栓：猫心肌病相关主动脉血栓；静脉血栓：免疫介导性溶血性贫血、肾病综合征等高凝状态。

**处理原则**
- 动脉血栓以抗血小板药为一线；静脉血栓以抗凝药为一线；高风险疾病可联合，获益与出血风险须个体化权衡。
- 用药期间监测红细胞压积、血小板与出血征象；华法林因治疗指数窄、个体差异大，已不推荐常规使用。

**涉及药物**
- 抗血小板：阿司匹林、氯吡格雷；抗凝：利伐沙班、低分子肝素（达肝素、依诺肝素）、普通肝素；溶栓：阿替普酶。

## 4.7 免疫介导性血液病（IMHA / IMTP）

**常见症状**
- IMHA：黏膜苍白、黄疸、乏力，可见发热、脾大；末梢血涂片见球形红细胞、自身凝集。
- IMTP：皮肤黏膜瘀点瘀斑、鼻出血、牙龈出血、黑便、血尿，可伴贫血。

**处理原则**
- 先完善免疫破坏证据检查（球形红细胞、生理盐水凝集试验、Coombs 试验等），并排查继发因素（肿瘤、感染、炎症、药物、疫苗）。
- 一线糖皮质激素免疫抑制；危重、依赖输血或激素不耐受时加用辅助免疫抑制剂；确诊即评估血栓预防，持续至缓解并停用激素。
- 重度贫血输压积红细胞；危及生命的血小板减少输富血小板全血；血小板恢复后逐步减药。

**涉及药物**
- 免疫抑制：泼尼松 / 泼尼松龙、霉酚酸酯、环孢素、硫唑嘌呤（犬）、苯丁酸氮芥（猫）、长春新碱、免疫球蛋白（IVIG）。
- 血栓预防：肝素、阿司匹林、氯吡格雷。

### 常用于诊断心脏病的影像学检查

| --- |
|X-rays|
|X-rays (also called radiographs) of the chest frequently help diagnose heart disease in pets. Finding generalized enlargement of the heart or enlargement of specific heart chambers makes the presence of heart disease more likely. The images may also provide clues as to the specific disease present. For example, fluid in the lungs is a common finding in congestive heart failure. Although chest x-rays are useful in evaluating patients with heart disease, they have certain limitations. The presence of fluid in the lungs does not definitively confirm a disease originating from the heart or exclude another origin, such as pulmonary (lung) disease. Also, assessment of overall heart size and the size of specific heart chambers is typically far less accurate than testing by echocardiography (ultrasonography).|
|Electrocardiography|
|Electrocardiography is the recording of the heart’s electrical activity from the body surface with the use of electrodes. It can be used to identify heart arrhythmias, such as bradycardia (slower than expected rhythm), tachycardia (faster than expected rhythm), or other abnormalities of rhythm (such as sinus arrhythmia or sinus arrest). Electrocardiography can also detect conduction disturbances, or failures of the electrical signals that cause the heart to contract to pass through the heart tissue. These include first-, second-, and third-degree atrioventricular block. Finally, electrocardiography can identify chamber enlargement, which is indicated by waveform abnormalities shown on the electrocardiogram recording. Different readings suggest enlargement of the different chambers. While the electrocardiogram may suggest chamber enlargement, chest x-rays and echocardiography (ultrasonography) are more effective.|
|Echocardiography|
|Echocardiography is a type of ultrasonography used to evaluate the heart, the aorta, and the pulmonary artery. Echocardiography complements other diagnostic procedures by examining and displaying the working heart and moving images of its action. Heart chamber and wall dimensions can be determined; the physical structure and motion of valves can be seen; and pressure differences, blood flow volumes, and several measurements of heart function can be calculated. There are 3 main types of echocardiography: 2-dimensional, m-mode, and Doppler. Two-dimensional echocardiography provides a wedge-shaped, 2-dimensional video of the heart as it moves. M-mode echocardiography is produced by a 1-dimensional beam of ultrasound that penetrates the heart, providing an “ice-pick view.” Differences in tissue density that are detected by the beam are then plotted on a screen. This is typically used to measure chamber size, wall thickness, valve motion, and the size of the aorta and pulmonary artery. Doppler echocardiography detects changing frequencies of the ultrasonic beam as it contacts moving red blood cells to measure the speed of blood flow and thus identify turbulent or high-speed flow. This can locate heart murmurs.|
|Cardiac Catheterization|
|Cardiac catheterization involves the placement of specialized catheters (thin, flexible tubes) into the heart, aorta, or pulmonary artery. Cardiac catheterization may be performed when other diagnostic tests are insufficient to identify specific heart abnormalities or are unable to identify the severity of a lesion. It may also be used for presurgical evaluation, treatment, and in clinical research. Diagnostic and presurgical cardiac catheterization, however, have largely been replaced by echocardiography (see above). Most of the time now, cardiac catheterization is done to surgically repair defects in the heart.|
